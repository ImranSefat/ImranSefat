###### STATEMENT OF SIGNIFICANCE

I'm a software engineer at the [Powerhouse Museum](https://powerhouse.com.au) in Sydney. I build the museum's website and ticketing, and work on its online collection. I also teach: more than a thousand people started their software careers in my classes, and I keep going on [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/c/CodingwithImran) and in [writing](https://medium.com/@CodingWithImran/).

I go by "Imran" or "Imran Sefat".
